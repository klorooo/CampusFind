About Us

Started with one folding table and a case of Nebbiolo.

Terroir & Co. began in 2014 when our founders, Mara and Elias, drove a rented van through Piedmont and came home with more questions than wine. Eleven years later we import from thirty-four growers across ten regions — but we still pack the boxes ourselves.

What we believe

Three rules we don't break

01

Taste first

We don't list a wine we haven't tasted at the source. Sell sheets and scores don't get a bottle onto our shelves.

02

Pay growers fairly

We agree prices before the harvest, not after — so a bad weather year doesn't become the grower's problem alone.

03

Keep the list small

We'd rather carry thirty wines we can vouch for personally than three hundred we can't.
